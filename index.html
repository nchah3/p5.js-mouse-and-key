Haunted Mirror

A little noise wakes the ghosts.

Move the mouse: guide a ghost  |  Click the canvas: play a spooky tone
C: change mirror color  |  Space: pause/resume animation  |  M: mute click tones  |  R: reset animation

Allow webcam and microphone access when prompted. Click the canvas before using the keys.
The bottom line shows whether the animation is paused and which key was received.
Use an HTTPS page or localhost.
The sketch uses live video and sound levels without recording or uploading them.
