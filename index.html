Haunted Mirror

A little noise wakes the ghosts.

Move the mouse: guide a ghost  |  Click the canvas: play a spooky tone
C: change mirror color  |  Space: Pause ghost motion  |  M: mute tones  |  R: reset animations

Allow webcam and microphone access when prompted. Use an HTTPS page or localhost.
The sketch uses live video and sound levels without recording or uploading them.
